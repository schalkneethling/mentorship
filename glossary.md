# Glossary

Terms we have learned, defined in our own words. Keep the list in
alphabetical order, one term per line.

- **Branch**: A separate line of work that leaves `main` untouched until it is merged.
- **Commit**: A saved snapshot of changes, with a message explaining why they were made.
- **Merge**: Combining the changes from one branch into another.
- **Pull request**: A request to merge a branch, where others can review the changes first.
- **Repository**: A project folder that remembers its whole history.
- **Working tree**: The files as they are on your computer right now, including changes not yet committed.
